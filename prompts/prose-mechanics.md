## Prose mechanics (final book prose)

- Direct internal thought, in the character's own words, goes in italics marked with single asterisks: *Keep walking,* she told herself. *Don't look back.* Reported thought stays plain: She told herself to keep walking.
- Italics are also for words a character reads or writes on the page. Use them for nothing else, and never use double asterisks or any other markup.
- A question a character puts to themselves ends with a question mark, even in narration: Why the north road? Why tonight? Only a statement that is not a question ends with a period.
